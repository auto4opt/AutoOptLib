# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone participating in the AutoOptLib community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not individuals.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different backgrounds
  and levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and deliberate
disruption are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to zhaoq@sustech.edu.cn. Maintainers may edit
or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is harmful to the community.
